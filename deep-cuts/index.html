Three Rutgers–New Brunswick prompts a day. Name a correct answer. The fewer Knights who say it, the more points you get.

How scoring works

One answer per prompt. Type any correct answer. A guess that isn't on the list costs a strike, and three strikes scores that prompt 0.

Points come from rarity. Your answer's share is the percent of players who gave the same answer. Points = 15 × log₂(1 ÷ share), capped at 100. If 50% of players say it, you get 15. At 6% you get 61. At 1% or less you get the full 100.

Where the percentages come from. Each answer starts with an estimate of how often a Rutgers student would say it. That estimate counts as 25 imaginary players. Every real player's answer is then added on top. Early in the day, the estimate does most of the work. After a few hundred players, the crowd decides almost everything. Scores update live, so the result is final at midnight Eastern.
